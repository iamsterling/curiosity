license: MIT

Define the interoperability question, legal and licensing boundary, and permitted evidence. Form small falsifiable hypotheses, test one at a time, and retain fixtures plus observations. Separate facts, inferences, and unknowns. Do not copy protected implementation, bypass access controls, or infer undocumented behavior as fact. Compare independent observations, record failed hypotheses, and end with a bounded conclusion or NO-GO. Use the deep-research bibliography and curiosity rules.
