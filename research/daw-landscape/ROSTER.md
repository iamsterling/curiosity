# DAW research roster

> Broad bounded census for documentary research. Inclusion does not endorse a
> product or assert that the roster contains every DAW ever released.

**Cutoff:** 2026-08-29 UTC
**Contract:** [`RESEARCH-CONTRACT.md`](RESEARCH-CONTRACT.md)
**Template:** [`DOSSIER-TEMPLATE.md`](DOSSIER-TEMPLATE.md)

## Inclusion and product-family rule

One independent owner researches one named DAW/product family and writes only
its unique dossier. Closely related editions on different platforms stay in one
dossier when they share a product identity; separately branded products with
materially different workflows receive separate dossiers. The roster spans
mainstream, post, open-source/Linux, tracker/modular, cloud, mobile, and
historically influential designs.
